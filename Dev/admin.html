Examination Cell Rules & Grievance SLA Guidelines

Standard operating procedures for marks re-evaluation, scrutiny, and hall ticket modifications.

What is the official resolution timeline for Re-evaluation?

Re-evaluation requests submitted through this portal are forwarded directly to the respective Department Head (HOD) within 24 hours. The entire process, including digital booklet re-checking and grade updates, is strictly completed within 7 working days.

What proof is required for missing practical/viva marks?

Please attach a PDF or image copy of your signed laboratory attendance log sheet or copy of the hall ticket signed by the external/internal supervisor. Max file size limit is 5MB per file.

How will I know when my issue is forwarded to a Professor?

When the Examination Cell Admin forwards your grievance to a faculty member, your grievance status will automatically update to 'Under Progress / Forwarded to Faculty' and you will receive a real-time notification alert detailing the faculty's name and forwarding message.
